## 5. Interpretation

The audit is designed to decide whether O2-B needs an additional scale or interval-length side case.  If the LongA negative envelope stays far below 1 and does not grow in the tail, then O2-B remains a centered bounded fluctuation theorem.

The result supports the centered-fluctuation framing.

The fully active LongA channel has

\[
\max_J[-z(J)]_+=0.0394632013,
\]

and in the post-\(500M\) tail,

\[
\max_{J,\ p^*\ge500M}[-z(J)]_+=0.0343723532.
\]

Every LongA row passes the much stronger empirical threshold

\[
[-z(J)]_+\le0.05,
\]

so the theorem envelope \([-z(J)]_+\le1\) has a factor \(>20\) of observed margin.

The coarse scale fit gives

\[
\text{slope in }p^*\approx -0.0403,
\]

with low \(R^2\), meaning no reliable growth trend is visible.  The \(h\)-bin fit is similarly weak and nearly flat:

\[
\text{slope in }h\approx0.0656.
\]

Thus the data does not indicate a scale-growing negative tail.  The negative LongA residual is frequent in some regimes, especially the tail, but its amplitude remains uniformly small.

Conclusion:

\[
\boxed{
\text{O2-B may be stated as a bounded centered LongA fluctuation theorem.}
}
\]

No additional \(p^*\)-scale, \(h\)-scale, depth, or \(\mu\)-regime exception is indicated by this audit.
